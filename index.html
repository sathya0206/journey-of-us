Chapter 1

School

Before there was an "us"...

When we were in 8th standard, I saw you on my usual way back home from school. That's the first scene I particularly remember, like I saw you for the first time.

I didn't get any of those cinematic effects when I saw you for the first time 😂, but the feeling I got was something like how they show in movies — love at first sight. That's how I remember it.

I know I was too small to have thought, "Iva dhan enaku pondaatti ah varanum... iva enaku pondaatti ah vandha epdi irukum?" 😂

That was the thought I had when I saw you at that time. I don't even remember whether I had seen you before or not, and I don't know why I got that feeling that day.

Maybe some destiny was already written by God. I don't know. But that feeling somehow stuck with me.

Somehow, We Ended Up in the Same Class

8th standard was over, and then we all got promoted to 9th. That's when the class shuffle happened.

Again, by some God's plan, we both were put into the same class. But I didn't have the same love feeling anymore because I was very strong about one thing — I should not love anyone.

That was the time when my sister's love got caught by my Mom, and there was a big drama going on at home. So I was very clear that I shouldn't get into any of this.

Days went by, and then my sister asked me to help her with their love story.

Then I had this thought: "Okay, what is wrong if I love someone?"

And the usual talks between friends started happening.

10th Standard Boys — Serious Discussion 😂

One day, Taraka and I were seriously discussing who should be our girlfriend.

10th standard boys' serious talk. 😂

He chose Smriti and I chose you because we were both around the same height. That's one reason I chose you at that time.

That Blue Chudidar

Then, all of a sudden, you appeared in my dream wearing your 10th standard birthday dress — that blue chudidar.

And that's when the feeling became strong again.

The Challenge

Somewhere between all this, someone came and challenged me, saying that I couldn't make you fall in love with me and that it was not possible.

Because apparently, if I approached you, you would prick me with your compass. 😂

So I had to be more careful.

I challenged him back: "Okay, don't put any time frame, but for sure I will make her love me."

It started as a game or something like that.

Later, I told you and you said no.

But by then, it had become serious for me.

Actually, that's when I realised, "Oh, I seriously like her."

I couldn't accept the thought of not talking to you anymore. I couldn't imagine that.

So I decided that somehow I had to make you love me. But before that, I had to somehow make you talk to me.

11B → 11A

Then we went on a long leave between 10th and 11th.

I still have the visuals in my mind of initially sitting in 11B while you were in 11A.

When the teacher came and asked who was willing to join 11A because there were two seats available, I was ready.

I'm the kind of person who usually hesitates to raise my hand when someone asks something like this.

But that time, I took my bag and stood up. 😂

I was ready to move to 11A because you were in that class, and I didn't want to leave the chance.

I still remember what you told me later — when you saw me coming from 11B to 11A, this was your exact reaction... ❤️

Those 3–4 Months

For almost 3–4 months, we didn't speak with each other.

Whenever I tried something silly, you would never react to it. But being a one-sided lover, even those little moments gave me butterflies. 😂

I still remember the first text you sent me after such a long time.

You know what I felt at that moment? It was something I cannot even explain in words.

That was the moment I gained confidence that, "Yes, this will work. She will love me back."

That one message somehow assured me of that.

Chess Competition Days

And then there were those chess competition days where I was always roaming behind you. 😂

For a long time, I would just sit peacefully and sight'adichufy you.

October 10 ❤️

Then came October 10.

You called me, and when you said "okay", I was jumping on my terrace. I was so immersed in happiness that I didn't even know what was happening around me. ❤️

Chapter 2

College

Distance, little moments and growing together.

Days slowly went by. There was lots of love, but there were some problems too. But I think those tough situations are what made our love stronger than we probably realise.

The distance and the long gaps between meeting each other somehow made our bonding even stronger.

We completed our 12th, and again there was a long gap where we didn't speak for many days.

I was worried when I heard that you had moved to Kerala. That was when you texted me one morning, telling me about the situation and saying that you would text or call me back when everything was okay and when nobody was around you.

That message gave me some relaxation. At least I knew you were okay.

Then you came back here and we both joined college. We would wait and meet at the bus stop on most days.

We would just roam around our area, spend some time together, and then you would go back home.

We would usually meet only once in around three months. I mean, going out and spending proper time together was actually very rare.

But somehow, we always managed to see each other. We would put in the effort, make a plan and somehow find a way to spend some time together.

We were always there for each other in all kinds of situations.

You would even walk every day and save your ₹10 for my birthday so that you could buy me a gift.

That meant a lot to me. More than the gift itself, the effort you put in to give me something was much bigger.

It was never about what you gave me. It was always about the effort you put in for me. ❤️

Whenever we fight, namma pesama irundhadhu illa. Sanda potute naalum, thittite naalum, namma pesite irukanum. That's how we are even today. ❤️

Our movie was Chennai Express. I can still recall that moment even now. 😂

We shared so many stories and talked about so many things. You were hesitant at first about some things, but later you became comfortable with me.

I still don't know how you trusted me like that or how you loved me this much.

Sometimes I genuinely go crazy thinking about it. Somehow, you still managed all my lunatic behaviour and my psychotic behaviour. 😂

When we went to Ronav's house on December 26, 2015, if I'm not wrong, I had a small surprise waiting for you when you entered the house.

You were shocked and smiling, and that whole moment is still very clearly in my memory.

Those were some of the best memories we made together.

And there is one thing I never really understood — how you would cry every time you had to leave and go back home.

I would be happy thinking, "At least we got to spend some time together," while you would be like, "Ahh, our together time is going to end."

Even now, I sometimes wonder what I did to deserve someone who loves me this much.

What did I ever do to you that made you love me this much and trust me so blindly? ❤️

Chapter 3

Work & Distance

When life wasn't easy, you were still there.

Once I finished college, I bought my first bike — the Hornet. That bike gave us endless memories.

I still remember taking you on that bike and dropping you at college for the first time.

Our longest ride on that bike was probably when we went to the Sriperumbudur temple. That was the longest trip we had on the Hornet, and even now it feels special when I think about it.

Then came the time when I was jobless.

You never put any pressure on me. You never pressured me or scolded me for being jobless. You always backed me, whatever the situation was.

Even though I didn't have a job, you treated me the same way you treat me now.

You always believed in me more than I believed in myself. ❤️

I know you had a fear inside you too. "What if the marriage talks start now?"

But even with that fear, you never put any of it on me.

I think that was a kind of understanding you developed at that time, and it really helped me indirectly because I was already going through a lot mentally.

When I didn't even know what I wanted to do with my life, you trusted me.

You were there for me then, and you continue to be there for me even now.

I know what I ask from you most of the time, and you have always made sure I was satisfied with that specific thing.

I can understand how hard it can be or how irritating it might have been for you sometimes, but you never showed any of that to me.

You adapted yourself and did what I asked.

We have talked about it so many times, but still, you never said no.

And I'm not talking only about this one thing. I mean in all the situations, all the time, you stood by me.

You trusted me, loved me and cared for me. ❤️

The days when I was in Coimbatore would have definitely been hard days for you.

I can still recall how sad you were and how much you cried when I told you that I had to join the office in Coimbatore.

We were both eagerly waiting for the day when I would come back to Chennai so that we could meet somewhere and spend some time together.

Adhellam nenacha ipo kooda viyappa irukum.

Basically, we went through some really hard days and still managed to stay in a relationship.

We travelled through so many years together, and there are still so many more years to come.

And then COVID came and tested our relationship again.

Even though it was a short-distance relationship, we still didn't see each other for almost a year, if I'm not wrong.

Again, we had to go through those long days without seeing each other.

Whenever I tell someone about our story, the first thing they usually ask is, "How did you guys manage without seeing each other for so long and still stay in a relationship?"

Everyone wondered how we managed it.

And honestly, even I wonder about it sometimes when I think about our love now. ❤️

Chapter 4

Marriage ❤️

From you and me to building a life together.

And finally, you made your parents accept our marriage.

There were a lot of problems with a few things, but somehow we managed all of them.

And then we got married on this very special day, four years ago.

21/08/2022 ❤️

A girl who had always been with her Mom. Her mother was her whole world. You trusted only your Mom, and whatever the situation was, all you wanted was your mother.

That kind of person came with me to my home after marriage.

Honestly, that was incredible to me.

Enaku okay sonnapo nee sonnadhu nyabagam iruku — "Unna nambi dhan okay solren. En appa vandhu against love... adhellam pidikadhu. Edhu naalum nee kooda irukanum."

And just like that, you came to my home trusting me.

I honestly don't know how you trusted me like that. I don't know how you loved me like that.

From where did you get all this love for me?

Idhu edhachu pona jenmam bandhama ennanu therila. ❤️

A lot of people tell me, "You are lucky enough to have me in your life."

But I think it goes both ways.

We both are lucky to have each other in our lives. ❤️

The emotional support I get from you, the love, the care... ellam chance eh illa.

I don't think I can ever properly explain how much all of that means to me.

Chapter 5

Life After Marriage

Four years of little moments that became our life.

In these four years, we have had endless memories.

One of the trips that holds a special place in my heart is our trip to Ooty.

I know it was a short trip, but somehow those three days gave me such a peace of mind.

It was just us. No disturbance, nothing else to think about. We were simply living our own life for those three days, without worrying about anything else.

Just you and me, living our own little life. ❤️

I don't know how we managed to create so many secret memories during those nights. 😂

Most of the time we would just be talking or getting ready to sleep, and somehow the whole mood would suddenly change.

And just like that, we would end up creating another little memory that only belongs to us. ❤️

We also had a lot of struggles financially.

It was always a question mark whether we would be able to save money or not.

But we were determined to become better as a couple.

Yeah, we are moving a little slowly, but we are on our own race, at our own pace.

And we are moving happily with whatever we have got.

That's one of the biggest plus points we have got. ❤️

Draping a saree for you is one of the most difficult tasks I have ever had. 😂

Because I have to do it quickly, but at the same time I shouldn't get distracted, and it should actually look good on you.

That's a really difficult task. 😂

Those snapshot photos you took with me and of us used to irritate me so much at that time.

But when I look at them now, they look funny and somehow give me a really good feeling.

Even the Kesariya song feels more special to me because of your Instagram post.

Luckily, whenever we went to some event, we somehow managed to take some good photos.

Eventually, many of those photos ended up becoming our little post-wedding photoshoot. ❤️

And then we decided to expand our family.

Two was becoming three. 🤰🏼❤️

But even while becoming three, we constantly found ourselves missing "US".

That's the bond we have developed over all these years.

And that's actually the reason why I started this story from school.

Even though it has been only four years since we got married, I think our relationship actually started when we fell in love with each other.

We have been in a continuous relationship from that very first day — through every moment and every situation.

I think we built this from a very long time ago. It is not just these four years.

These successful four years are because of the foundation we built long before we got married. ❤️

That's why I wanted to go through some of our old memories too.

We have fought a lot.

But we never kept things in our minds for long. We resolved them immediately and didn't take them to heart.

We were always more focused on our relationship and our love.

That's why we were able to build this kind of bonding between us.

More than anything else, we always put our love first. ❤️

Chapter 6

From Two to Three ❤️

And somehow, our two became three.

Many times you have asked me how much I love you.

There are certain things you simply cannot measure — the depth of an ocean, how long the sky is, how hot the Sun is.

Similarly, I cannot measure the love I have for you.

You will always be my first baby, and Anvi will always be my first daughter. So you will always hold a special place in my heart — before, now and always. ❤️

I still wonder about the courage you had to enter pregnancy.

How you handled the pregnancy, carrying our child inside you, working until the 8th month, and even taking injections when you were afraid of them.

Even now, I get that shocking feeling when I remember seeing you go into the operation theatre.

Even though you didn't have complete courage at that moment, you still entered there for our baby.

You have been there for her from the very first day.

Even when you were going through so much physically and mentally, you came with her to the hospital, carried her, and cried when she cried.

I have seen you handle everything much better than you probably realise.

You are much stronger than you think. ❤️

After all these years of being with you, you have never disappointed me in any of the roles you have taken in my life.

Lover → Wife → Mother.

From two, we became three in four years.

I'm lucky to have you in my life as my life partner, lover, friend, wife, and in every possible role.

You have never judged me in any situation. You are the only one who has always understood me without making me feel judged.

I love you.
I love you sooo much. ❤️

You will always hold that special place in my life. You are that special person who came into my life and changed it forever.

You are my lucky charm. My precious. ❤️

"Iraivanai thandha iraiviye" is something that suits you perfectly.

God destined this for us.

Let's take that responsibility together — with more care, more love and more understanding, and take our relationship to the next next level.

There are many more years to come. So let's enjoy each and every moment.

We have a long way to go.

Let's promise each other that we will love each other more, care for each other, stay healthy, build wealth, and live our life to the maximum.

Happy, blessed and lucky to have you in my life. ❤️

Happy Anniversary, my precious. ❤️

And our story continues...
